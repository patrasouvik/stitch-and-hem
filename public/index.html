How it works

Four steps, no fuss

Most jobs need one visit in and one visit out. That's it.

1

Book a slot

Pick a day and rough time using the form below, or just walk in — we keep slots free.

2

Get pinned & priced

Bring the shoes and underlayers you'll wear. We pin, measure and give you a firm price.

3

We do the work

Sewn by hand and machine in our back room. We text you the moment it's ready.

4

Try it, love it

Try it on at collection. If anything's off, we adjust it again free of charge.
